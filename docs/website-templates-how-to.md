# Website Templates How-To

## Where to click

Manager Portal → Website & Pages → Website Templates

## Step 1: Choose a theme

1. Browse the template cards.
2. Profession affects recommendation and ordering, not hard availability.
3. Click **Preview** to inspect a theme without publishing it.
4. Click **Apply Style** to save the selected theme to draft.

## Step 2: Preview

1. Use the Builder Canvas or the signed draft preview.
2. Switch device preview as needed.
3. Draft preview reflects `settings_draft`.
4. Published live URLs reflect published settings only.

## Step 3: Keep your content

1. Theme switching preserves canonical pages, media, services, products, reviews, jobs, navigation, footer, and SEO.
2. A normal theme switch changes visual composition only.
3. Starter content/media installation is reserved for genuinely empty tenants.

Notes:
- Classic remains a separate legacy renderer path.
- Approved product themes are limited to the integrated Next.js theme library.
- Source-only themes on disk are not automatically exposed in the Builder.
